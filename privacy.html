旅貼隱私說明

最後更新：2026-11-02

你的帳目只存在你的裝置

旅程、帳目、備註和設定都保存在你這個瀏覽器的本機儲存裡，不會上傳到任何伺服器。旅貼沒有帳號，也沒有雲端同步，開發者看不到你的任何帳目。

備份檔由你保管

你在「備份與設定」匯出的 JSON 或 CSV 檔，只會存到你自己選的位置，內含你的私人旅程資料，請妥善保存。

匿名瀏覽統計

旅貼使用 Vercel Web Analytics 統計瀏覽次數，用來了解有多少人在用。它不使用 cookie，也不會讀取你輸入的帳目和備註，只收集匯總資料，例如瀏覽的頁面、來源網站、瀏覽器、作業系統、裝置類型和大概的國家或地區。

網站主機

旅貼的網站由 Vercel 提供主機服務。和所有網站一樣，主機在傳送檔案時會處理你的 IP 位址等連線資訊，用來提供服務和防止濫用。詳情請看 Vercel 隱私政策。

意見回饋

在「備份與設定」→「意見回饋」送出時，旅貼只會傳送你填的類型、內容，以及你自願填的名字和國家或地區。這些資料存放在開發者使用的 Cloudflare 資料庫，只用來改進旅貼。旅貼不會附上你的帳目，也不會把你的 IP 位址存進資料庫；Cloudflare 會短暫用 IP 來防止濫發。請不要在內容裡寫你不想讓開發者看到的個人資料。想刪除你送過的意見，可以再送一則回饋，說明大約的時間和內容。

贊助連結

設定頁的贊助按鈕（☕ 贊助旅貼）會在瀏覽器開啟第三方贊助平台。贊助與付款資料由該平台處理，旅貼本身不經手。

刪除資料

  • 刪除單一旅程：在貼紙簿打開旅程，按刪除。
  • 刪除全部資料：在瀏覽器設定中清除這個網站的資料。清除後無法復原，除非你有備份檔。

變更

這份說明如有修改，會更新本頁的日期。

Lvtie Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Your records stay on your device

Trips, records, notes and settings are saved in this browser’s local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to any server. Lvtie has no accounts and no cloud sync, and the developer cannot see any of your records.

You keep your backup files

JSON or CSV files you export from “Backup & Settings” are saved only where you choose. They contain your private trip data, so keep them safe.

Anonymous visit statistics

Lvtie uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits, so we know roughly how many people use Lvtie. It uses no cookies and never reads the records or notes you enter. It only collects aggregate data such as the page visited, referring site, browser, operating system, device type and approximate country or region.

Website hosting

Lvtie is hosted by Vercel. Like any website host, Vercel processes connection details such as your IP address to deliver the files and prevent abuse. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Feedback

When you send feedback from “Backup & Settings” → “Send feedback”, Lvtie sends only the type and message you entered, plus a name and country or region if you chose to add them. This is stored in a Cloudflare database used by the developer, only to improve Lvtie. None of your records are attached, and your IP address is not saved in the database; Cloudflare briefly uses it to prevent spam. Please don’t include personal details you don’t want the developer to see. To have feedback you sent deleted, send another note saying roughly when you sent it and what it said.

Support link

The support button in Settings opens a third-party support platform in your browser. Support and payment details are handled by that platform; Lvtie itself never receives them.

Deleting your data

  • One trip: open it in the sticker book and delete it.
  • Everything: clear this site’s data in your browser settings. This can’t be undone unless you have a backup file.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date on this page will be updated.
